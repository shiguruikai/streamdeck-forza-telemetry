# Become a Maker
Got an idea? Share it with the Elgato community and reach millions of new customers. Set your own price, build your brand, and earn cash across the globe.
A Maker is anyone who creates for [Marketplace](https://marketplace.elgato.com/), and we believe everyone can be a Maker!
!The Maker Logo
## Publishing
### Submission
Products are submitted using [Maker Console](https://maker.elgato.com/), a dedicated space for Makers that allows you to easily submit, manage, and update your products on Marketplace.
To get started:
  1. Head over to [Maker Console](https://maker.elgato.com/).
  2. Create your organization, e.g. Elgato, Corsair, etc.
  3. Sign the Maker Agreement.

Once in Maker Console, you can submit your first product for [review](review-process.md).
Maker Agreement
You must have created your organization in Maker Console and signed the Maker Agreement before submitting a product for review. 
## Supported Products
Marketplace supports listing Maker products for the following product types:
  * Stream Deck
    * Plugins
    * Profiles
    * Icons
    * Screensavers1
  * Wave Link
    * Audio effects (VSTs)1
    * EQ presets1
  * Camera Hub
    * LUTs1
    * Backgrounds1
  * OBS scene collections (Marketplace Connect)
  * 3D print files1

1 requires email submissions.
Submitting other product types
You can submit products not yet supported by Maker Console by emailing maker@elgato.com with the subject **"New Product Submission"** and include the following details:
  * **Name** —**** the name of your product as it will appear on the product page.
  * **Description** — minimum of a few sentences, up to 1,500 characters.
  * **Price** —**** if free, please state so. Otherwise, include your desired price and currency.
  * **Media** —**** 1 thumbnail and a minimum of 3 gallery images.
  * **Product files** — the files that make up your product.

Optionally, you may also include
  * **Additional links** — any relevant links to your website, support page, setup guide, demo, or privacy policy. 
  * **Video** —**** 1920 × 1080 in MP4 format (≤50 MB), with a thumbnail at 1920 × 960 in PNG or JPEG format.

## Country Limitations
Marketplace processes Maker payouts via Stripe Connect. Stripe Connect is available in many countries, however, if your country is not supported you will not be able to list paid products on Marketplace. You will still be able to list free products without restrictions.
[Learn more about monetization on Marketplace, and Stripe Connect](stripe.md)
Marketplace availability in restricted countries
Due to legal requirements and payment processing limitations, Marketplace is currently inaccessible in the following countries:
  * Bahrain
  * Cuba
  * Donetsk People's Republic
  * Iran
  * Jordan
  * Kuwait
  * Luhansk People's Republic
  * North Korea
  * Oman
  * Russia
  * Syria
  * Tunisia

For more information or questions, please [contact us](https://elgato.com/help).
## FAQ
**What is the payout split with Elgato?**
Marketplace operates on a 70/30 payment split, wherein you, the product owner, take 70% and Elgato takes 30%. For more information on payouts, please refer to our [Stripe Connect](https://docs.elgato.com/makers/general/getting-paid/stripe-connect) page.
**Does Elgato own my images / software once I list them with on Marketplace?**
No. Your remain the owner of all intellectual property you publish on Marketplace, and Elgato does not take ownership.
**Can I list my products in other stores, such as Etsy, etc?**
Yes. You may list your items elsewhere while you have them listed on Marketplace. We ask that you keep consistent pricing across all platforms.
**I need assistance. How do I get in touch with someone at Elgato?**
Join the [Marketplace Makers Discord Server](https://discord.gg/4rTB7cYzyj) to receive the latest Maker-related announcements, meet other Makers, ask developer questions, and get help creating products for the Stream Deck. You can also email us directly at maker@elgato.com.
