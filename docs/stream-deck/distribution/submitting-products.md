# Submitting Products
Products available on Marketplace are submitted and managed through Maker Console; here you can submit new products, manage existing products, and submit new versions or revisions.
On this page, you'll learn more about: 
  * Submitting your products using Maker Console.
  * Supported product types.
  * The review process.

## Supported Products
Marketplace supports listing Maker products for the following product types:
  * Stream Deck
    * Plugins
    * Profiles
    * Icons
    * Screensavers1
  * Wave Link
    * Audio effects (VSTs)1
    * EQ presets1
  * Camera Hub
    * LUTs1
    * Backgrounds1
  * OBS scene collections (Marketplace Connect)
  * 3D print files1

1 requires email submissions.
Submitting other product types
You can submit products not yet supported by Maker Console by emailing maker@elgato.com with the subject **"New Product Submission"** and include the following details:
  * **Name** —**** the name of your product as it will appear on the product page.
  * **Description** — minimum of a few sentences, up to 1,500 characters.
  * **Price** —**** if free, please state so. Otherwise, include your desired price and currency.
  * **Media** —**** 1 thumbnail and a minimum of 3 gallery images.
  * **Product files** — the files that make up your product.

Optionally, you may also include
  * **Additional links** — any relevant links to your website, support page, setup guide, demo, or privacy policy. 
  * **Video** —**** 1920 × 1080 in MP4 format (≤50 MB), with a thumbnail at 1920 × 960 in PNG or JPEG format.

Soundboard Submissions
If you are an IP holder and would like to become an approved soundboard partner, please reach out to us at maker@elgato.com.
## Uploading Your Product
To submit your product through Maker Console, you will need:
  * Your product's file
  * Name
  * Description
  * Thumbnail image — 1920 × 960 px PNG
  * 3 gallery items, either images and/or a video — 1920 × 960 px PNG, or 1920 × 1080 MP4
  * Additional links, such as support links or resources
  * Release notes

Review guidelines
Before submitting your product please review the necessary [product](https://docs.elgato.com/guidelines/products) and [branding](https://docs.elgato.com/guidelines/branding) guidelines, as well as guidelines specific to your product type. Failure to meet the quality expectations may result in delays to your product's publication.
warning
Your product's name and monetization options cannot be changed in Maker Console. Should you wish to change these, please contact us at maker@elgato.com.
When you have everything you need:
  1. Log in to [Maker Console](https://maker.elgato.com)
  2. Navigate to **Home**
  3. Select **Create product**

And follow the on-screen wizard.
### Step 1. Files
Select your **product type** , for example Stream Deck plugin.
!Step 1 — select your product type.
Upload your **product's file**
!Step 2 — upload your product file.
### Step 2. Details
Review your product's**name** and**description** — when writing your description, be sure to include keywords for the software, game, or devices your product is designed for. This helps with user discoverability, internal search results, and browser SEO.
!Step 3 — review your product's name and description.
Set the **tags** , **pricing** , and **additional links** — for products such as Stream Deck profiles and icons, tags allow you to specify the supported devices and operating systems, as well as tell users more about your product, such as theme and color etc.
info
The price specified in Maker Console is the product's net price.
!Step 4 — set your product's tags, price, and additional links.
### Step 3. Media and Release Notes
Upload your **product's media** including thumbnail and gallery items, and app icon (Stream Deck plugins) and icon previews (Stream Deck icons) where necessary.
!Step 5 — upload your product's media, including thumbnail and gallery items.
Add **release notes** , that detail what is included as part of these changes.
!Step 6 — review your product, add the release notes, and submit for review.
Lastly, **submit** your product for review!
Submission inquiries
For review and submission inquiries please contact us on maker@elgato.com.
